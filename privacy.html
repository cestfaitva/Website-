Privacy Notice

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data C’est Fait Virtual Assistance collects, why, and your rights. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to reply to you and to deliver our services.

Who we are. C’est Fait Virtual Assistance is run by Ruth Nzangi, a sole trader based in Liverpool, United Kingdom. For personal data collected through this website and for our own clients’ contact and billing details, we are the data controller.

Contact: hello@cestfaitva.com · 0333 070 4375

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, email, business or role, country or time zone, the services you’re interested in, hours, start date, preferred language and your message.
  • Emails and calls: whatever you choose to send us, and your phone number if you call.
  • Clients: contact details, contract details, invoices and payment records.

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis (UK GDPR)
Replying to your enquiry and preparing a quoteSteps at your request before a contract / legitimate interests
Delivering our services and managing the client relationshipContract

We never sell your data or use it for marketing without your consent.

Client data we handle for you

When we manage a client’s inbox, calendar or systems, the client is the controller of that data and we act as their processor, following their written instructions and the data protection terms in our client agreement.

Who we share it with

Only trusted providers we need to run the business:

  • FormSubmit, which delivers the enquiry form to our inbox, and sends you an automatic confirmation email.
  • Zoho Mail (email, hosted in the UK data centre).
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting) and Google Fonts (website fonts), which may process your IP address when you visit.
  • Rinkel (business phone line).
  • Our accountant and HMRC, where required by law.

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards UK law requires, such as adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t become clients: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client contract and billing records: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as HMRC requires.
  • Client data we handle as processor: deleted or returned when the agreement ends.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy to move elsewhere. Email hello@cestfaitva.com and we’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk · 0303 123 1113.

Security

We use password-protected devices, two-factor authentication and up-to-date software, and access client systems through delegated access rather than shared passwords wherever possible.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows the latest version.
